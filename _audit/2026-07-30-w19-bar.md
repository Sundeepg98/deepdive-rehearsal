## 1. X2b -- the constants were ten, not three, and one was already wrong

### What was there

The audit named three portrait constants (56 / 69 / 88) plus a landscape triplet (51 / 59 / 76).
Reading the source for the fix found **five per orientation** -- the audit's list missed `.app`'s
padding-bottom and `.scrolltop` entirely, and `.scrolltop`'s is the one that decides whether a
floating control lands **on** the bar it exists to dodge:

| consumer | portrait | landscape | what it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `styles.css:744` `.app{padding-top}` | **56px** | 51px | clear the fixed `.seg` strip |
| `styles.css:744` `.app{padding-bottom}` | 80px | 64px | clear the fixed `.mockcta` bar |
| `styles.css:1412` `.scrolltop{bottom}` | 80px + 16 | 64px + 10 | lift the FAB over the bar |
| `drill/logic.js:404/407` `scroll-margin-top` | 69px | 59px | the drill's seat, top |
| `drill/logic.js:404/407` `scroll-margin-bottom` | 88px | 76px | the drill's seat, bottom |

Every one of them decomposes as **(the bar) + (an authored gap)** -- and once decomposed, nine of
the ten are exactly right and one is not:

```
portrait   .seg = 61   .mockcta = 72        landscape  .seg = 51   .mockcta = 60
  padTop     56  = 61 - 5   <-- THE DEFECT     padTop     51  = 51 + 0
  padBot     80  = 72 + 8                      padBot     64  = 60 + 4
  fab        96  = 72 + 24                     fab        74  = 60 + 14
  seatTop    69  = 61 + 8                      seatTop    59  = 51 + 8
  seatBot    88  = 72 + 16                     seatBot    76  = 60 + 16
```

`.app{padding-top:56px}` against a strip measuring 61 is not a gap of -5, it is an error, and it put
the first **5px of `.side-id` under the fixed bar at every phone width, in the gate's own engine**.

### What replaced it

`src/scripts/app/chrome-metrics.js` publishes two custom properties on `<html>` from the live boxes:

- `--chrome-top` -- what fixed chrome steals from the top (`.seg`)
- `--chrome-bot` -- what it steals from the bottom (`.mockcta`)

"Stolen", not "height": only a **position:fixed, rendered** bar costs the content anything. That
definition is copied verbatim from `test/fold_budget.cjs`'s live-band computation, deliberately --
the app and the check that guards it must not be able to disagree about what chrome means. Values
are **ceil**ed, never floored: reserving less than the bar occupies is the defect being removed.

Every consumer now states the bar plus its own gap. The gaps stay authored, because a gap is a
design decision that does not desync when an engine changes the bar; a bar height typed into a
stylesheet is the thing that does. Portrait and landscape gaps differ on purpose (8 vs 4 above the
bar, 24 vs 14 for the FAB) -- the short-viewport block exists to give height back.

**The drill's four numbers are deleted, not re-expressed.** One measured pair reproduces all four:

| | portrait | landscape |
|---|---|---|
| `calc(var(--chrome-top) + var(--space-8))` | 61 + 8 = **69** | 51 + 8 = **59** |
| `calc(var(--chrome-bot) + var(--space-16))` | 72 + 16 = **88** | 60 + 16 = **76** |

The entire portrait/landscape difference was the bar re-padding itself; with the bar measured, the
landscape `scroll-margin` override at `logic.js:407` had nothing left in it and is gone. `.app`'s
`padding-top` override went the same way -- `var(--chrome-top)` is exact in both orientations.

**`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is no longer written separately, and that is not a regression.**
`.mockcta` spends the inset in its own `padding-bottom`, so the measured `--chrome-bot` already
contains it. On a device with a 34px inset the old form gave `80 + 34 = 114` and the new gives
`(72 + 34) + 8 = 114`. Same answer; adding `env()` on top of the measurement would have been the
double count.

### Receipts

| @ Chromium 149, dpr2, asserted viewport | 360x844 before | 360x844 after | 844x390 before | 844x390 after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.seg` / `.mockcta` measured | 61 / 72 | 61 / 72 | 51 / 60 | 51 / 60 |
| `--chrome-top` / `--chrome-bot` | *(unset)* | **61px / 72px** | *(unset)* | **51px / 60px** |
| `.app` padding-top / -bottom | 56px / 80px | **61px** / 80px | 51px / 64px | 51px / 64px |
| **`.side-id` hiddenTop** | **5.0px** | **0** | 0 | 0 |
| `.scrolltop` bottom | 96px | 96px | 74px | 74px |
| drill seat (`.thread`) | 69 / 88 | 69 / 88 | 59 / 76 | 59 / 76 |
| drill seat (`#adv`) | **0 / 0** | **69 / 88** | **0 / 0** | **59 / 76** |

Nine of the ten values are unchanged to the pixel; the 5px correction is the only movement, and
`#adv` acquires the seat it never had (which is what makes X7 fixable at all).

## 2. The re-derive cases, each measured rather than assumed

A `ResizeObserver` on both bars, **`{box:'border-box'}`**, plus a `matchMedia('(max-width:919px)')`
listener for the fixed -> static flip at 920px (a position change at an unchanged height is the one
thing a ResizeObserver cannot see). Writes are guarded on a real change, so a re-derive that
measures the same bar touches no style and the observer cannot chase its own tail.

| case | instrument | result |
|---|---|---|
| **orientation** | 360x844 -> 844x390 -> back, one page, no reload | `--chrome-top/bot` 61/72 -> **51/60** -> 61/72; `.app` padding follows; hiddenTop 0 throughout |
| **text size** | CDP `Page.setFontSizes` standard 16 -> 28 | bar **unchanged at 61** -- and that is a fact about the bar, not a gap in the observer: its height is the 44px tap floor + 8px padding x2 + 1px border, not a line box. The app's own A-/A+ control cannot move it either (`styles.css:1373` hides `.textzoom` below 920px, and it scales `.stage`'s zoom, never `.sidebar`). |
| **drill setup disclosure** | `dsu-closed` removed | bars **unmoved** (61/72), as expected -- the disclosure is in the drill's shadow root, both bars are in `.sidebar`. Re-derives anyway and costs nothing. |
| **a taller bar from any cause** | `.seg{padding-top:20px}` -> 73px | `--chrome-top` **73**, `.app` padding-top **73**, drill seat **81**, hiddenTop **0**. Reverts cleanly when the cause does. |

**The scrollbar route does not reproduce in Chromium and is recorded as such.** Giving the strip its
scrollbar back (`::-webkit-scrollbar{display:block;height:12px}`, with and without
`scrollbar-width:auto` and `scrollbar-gutter:stable`) leaves `.seg` at 61/g1 in all three variants
-- Chromium will not reserve that gutter here, which is exactly why X2 was invisible to this gate by
construction. The **padding** plant is the faithful stand-in: it is the mechanism the app's own
landscape block uses, and it produces `.seg = 73px`, which is precisely the height WebKit 26.5
reported when its scrollbar did reserve space inside the strip.

**That plant earned its keep immediately.** The first draft of `chrome-metrics.js` used a default
(content-box) ResizeObserver. Under the padding plant the bar went 61 -> 73 and the observer **never
fired**: these bars are sized by a 44px tap floor that padding sits outside of, so the content box is
44px in both orientations and does not move when the bar does. It had appeared to work only because
an orientation change also changes the bar's **width**, which the content box does report. That draft
passed every other arm of the new check. Measured on the base build, the same plant reproduces the
audit's WebKit number exactly: `.seg` 73, **`.side-id` hiddenTop 17px**, `.app` padding stuck at 56.

## 3. X7 -- the seat reached stage 2 and abandoned the way forward

### What the measurement actually showed

The audit filed one instance at 390x844. Measured across three topics x four phone viewports, fresh
context each, real hit-tested tap on Reveal, scroll at rest: **9 of 12 buried**, three of them
returning the Mock-run bar to a hit test at the control's own centre.

| 360x800 / 360x844 / 390x844 / 414x896 | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `content-pipeline` | `[723.7,767.7]` **90%** hit `BUTTON#mockopen` / `[723.7,767.7]` 0% ok / `[680.8,724.8]` 0% ok / `[654.4,698.4]` 0% ok | all four **in band**, hit `BUTTON#adv.push.more` |
| `notifications` | `[737.2,781.2]` **121%** hit `SPAN.mb-lbl` / `[781.2,825.2]` **121%** / `[780.3,824.3]` **119%** / `[832.9,876.9]` **120%** | all four **in band**, hit `BUTTON#adv.push.more` |
| `debugging` | `[855.1,899.1]` **389%** / `[855.1,899.1]` **289%** / `[782.9,826.9]` **125%** / `[833.6,877.6]` **122%** | all four **in band**, hit `BUTTON#adv.push.more` |

**12 of 12 in band after, 12 of 12 hit tests landing on the control itself, occlusion 0% throughout.**

### The fix, and why it is not `stage >= 1` alone

W2's stage-1 reasoning is **correct and kept verbatim**: the new block at stage 1 is `.ans`, and
seating that at the top of the band would scroll the probe off its own screen. The gap it left is
that the **block** and the **control** are different elements with different needs. So the stage-2
`block:'start'` seat is untouched, and stage 1 gets a different one -- `block:'nearest'` on `#adv`,
which scrolls only if the control is out of the band and only as far as needed. That is what
preserves W2's constraint instead of trading it away: the minimum scroll keeps the maximum of the
just-revealed answer on screen, where `block:'start'` on `.ans` would have thrown the question away
by construction.

It works **only because X2b gave `#adv` a `scroll-margin-bottom`**. Without one the browser seats it
flush against a viewport edge it believes is at 844 and the user sees as the top of the mock bar.
The two halves of this wave are one mechanism, which is why they were bundled.

**Stage 0 stays unseated, deliberately.** `renderD()` calls `drawCard(0, land)` with `land` **true**
whenever a redraw destroyed the focused element -- jumping to a probe from the nav, or grading. A
stage-0 seat would therefore fire on a **fresh probe** and scroll past the question the pane exists
to ask, undoing W2's entire fold budget.

### Measured and deliberately not fixed

**Landscape 844x390, stage 0, before any interaction:** `#adv` at `[337.8,381.8]` against a band
ending at 330 -- **118% occluded, hit test returns `SPAN.mb-lbl`** -- on 2 of 3 topics. This
reproduces the audit's own landscape receipt (`[338.7,382.7]`) to within 1px, and it is
**byte-identical before and after this wave**. It is not fixed because it is a *budget* problem, not
a *seat* problem: it arrives on entry, where a scroll would fight `fold_budget`'s guarantee that the
question's first line is on screen at scrollY 0. Below the fold on arrival is normal web layout;
buried after the app moved the screen for you is not.

**Note this is also why the roadmap's suggested `stage >= 2` -> `stage >= 1` would not have covered
it** -- stage 0 is not `>= 1` either way. The landscape case needs a band, not a gate.

**The judgment row** (`.judge`, 438px below the fold at the grading point) is likewise untouched:
the audit recorded it and pointedly did not file it, because reading the senior-signal block before
grading is plausibly the intent.

## 4. Guards, and both watched RED on `2de8bef`

**`test/chrome_metrics.cjs`** (new, 20 arms). Asserts the **relationship**, never a number: what the
app reserves == what the bar takes, in both orientations, for all five consumers, with the authored
gaps stated as themselves. It also strips the inline style and requires `styles.css`'s **fallback**
pair to equal the measured truth -- those two pairs are the last constants in the system, and a
fallback nobody checks is a constant with a better name. Desktop is asserted **out** of the system
(both vars 0, both paddings 0 at 1280x800), which is what keeps every desktop baseline out of the
blast radius. Plants a taller bar and **aborts** unless every derived value follows.

> RED on `2de8bef`: **17 of 20 arms fail**, and the plant aborts. Sample: `sideIdHiddenTop 5`
> (`{"padTop":56,"segH":61}`), `#adv` seat `{"adv":[0,0],"content":[69,88]}`, fallback pair
> `{"top":null,"bot":null}`.

**`test/fold_budget.cjs` + 6 X7 arms + 1 plant.** The right home: it is the same measurement
(geometry relative to the live fixed chrome), invisible to every behaviour check and to VR, and the
band comes from the same `FOLD()` probe. The **hit test is the assertion**, not the rectangle --
"below the fold" is normal web layout; a control whose centre belongs to another widget is not. Its
plant undoes the seat, which *is* the shipped build's resting state.

> RED on `2de8bef`: **all 6 arms fail** with hits on `BUTTON#mockopen.mockbtn`, `SPAN.mb-lbl` and
> `null`; the plant then aborts (on a build with no seat, "undo the seat" is a no-op).

### Two harness traps found and closed, both timing, both silent

1. **`router.js:234-246` pins the page to scrollY 0 for 400ms after boot**, with
   `scroll-behavior:auto`. A reveal inside that window has its seat silently undone. Not a product
   defect -- no thumb reaches Reveal within 400ms of boot -- but it cost a full measurement round in
   which the same viewport and topic passed or failed depending on how fast the box happened to
   boot. The arms now wait for the **condition** (the router handing `scroll-behavior` back).
2. **The X7 plant carried its own copy of the drill-entry sequence** and was missing the
   topic-switch wait, so it tapped Reveal mid-switch and `view-manager.js:87`'s `scrollTo(0,0)` undid
   the seat -- aborting a gate run whose six real arms had all passed, **only under load**. One
   `enterDrillOn()` helper now serves both so they cannot drift apart (commit `2616777`).

A third timing trap was in the throwaway sweep, not the repo, and is recorded because it nearly
produced a false verdict: a fixed 400ms wait after the tap samples the **smooth** seat scroll
mid-flight and reads it as "did not fire". `fold_budget`'s own header already warned about exactly
this; the arms use its `settleScroll` (three agreeing samples).

## 5. VR -- the two pre-authorized baselines, attributed

`m-walk-light` and `m-walk-dark` re-captured. **The other 14 are byte-identical**, confirmed twice:
0 changed px in the compare run, and 14 unchanged `sha256` fields in `manifest.json` after
`--update`. Every rule this wave touched is inside `@media(max-width:919px)`.

The churn is a **5px downward translation of the content band and nothing else**, proven by
comparing the new capture's row `y` against the old baseline's row `y - shift`:

| | `m-walk-light` | `m-walk-dark` |
|---|---|---|
| fixed `.seg` strip, rows 0-60, shift 0 | **0 / 23790 px differ** | **0 / 23790** |
| fixed `.mockcta`, rows 772-843, shift 0 | **0 / 28080 px differ** | **0 / 28080** |
| content band, shift 0 | 59659 (21.5%) | 55807 (20.1%) |
| content band, shift 4 | 34685 (12.6%) | 27553 (10.0%) |
| **content band, shift 5** | **8188 (2.97%)** | **1134 (0.41%)** |
| content band, shift 6 | 36165 (13.2%) | 27408 (10.0%) |

Shift 5 is a sharp minimum with 4x-24x separation from its neighbours, and **both fixed bars are
byte-identical at shift 0** -- they are `position:fixed`, so `.app`'s padding cannot move them, which
is the control that makes the attribution mean something.

The residual is fully accounted for: it lies **entirely in rows 746-771** (light) and 756-771 (dark)
of 844 -- 24 and 12 rows respectively, full width, channel deltas median 5 / max 8 and median 11 /
max 40. That band is the bottom bar's up-cast `box-shadow:0 -8px 24px`. The shadow does **not** move;
the content sliding 5px beneath it re-composites. A consequence of the shift, not a second change.

The new baseline was reviewed as an image, not only as numbers: the topic identity row
(`MSG | Event-Driven Back...`) now begins clear of the strip instead of 5px under it.

**One build-time trap worth recording:** `src/scripts/app.js` is a list of build-include comments
that is inlined **into a `<script>`**, where `<!--` comments to end of line only. A wrapped
multi-line HTML comment there parses as JavaScript -- it took the whole app down with
`Unexpected identifier 'to'`, and `syntax_check.py` could not see it because the source modules all
parse individually. The note is one line now, and says why.
