## 2. THE TWO TAIL FIXES, WITH THEIR MEASUREMENTS

### T1 -- THE HOME PRINT

Cycle 4's R5 stopped `print-qa.js` from taking Ctrl+P on the home -- correctly, because
`openPrint()` read `TopicRegistry.current()`, the BOOT constant on a route with no topic. Handing
the route back to the browser's own print was only half an answer: nobody asked what the browser
prints there. Driven at 1280x900, A4, `preferCSSPageSize`, on the shipped build:

```
home, fresh (no cram ever opened)          3 pages / 391,415 bytes  -- the BOOT topic's cram sheet
home, after visiting saga's sheet, closed  6 pages / 872,924 bytes  -- SAGA's cram sheet
AFTER THE FIX                              2 pages / 143,721 bytes  -- the record itself
```

Two causes one line apart in `@media print`: `.app{display:none !important}` (right for a topic
route, whose panes are shadow DOM and print blank -- the entire reason the cram substitution
exists) and the cram force-show carrying no `.open` requirement, so a CLOSED dialog printed.

**The named fix was `body:not(.print-session) .cram-ov.open`, and it was DEVIATED FROM, with the
measurement that forced it.** That is the right sentence one scope too wide: on a TOPIC route the
never-opened sheet IS the printable artifact, and `print_truth.cjs` ARM F owns it as a P1. Built
with the wider form:

```
#walk, cram never opened:  3 pages / 391,447 bytes  ->  1 page / 1,048 bytes
print_truth:  FAIL [file-print] a never-opened sheet still renders to paper  sections=0
```

Scoped to the home instead. `:not(.open)` is kept rather than flattened because an OPEN sheet on
the home is a REACHABLE state -- driven both doors (`location.hash` and the Home button), a topic
left with the sheet up lands on `#home` with `.cram-ov.open` still true and on screen.

The chrome hidden with it was derived from the page. `.hm-rail` and `.hm-tabs` are `position:fixed`
bars at the 680px A4 content box and would repeat on every sheet; `.hm-skip` is a preference
checkbox. `#scrolltop` was checked and LEFT (opacity 0, and a child of `<body>`, not `.app`).
`.hm-status` is the one that is not chrome -- it carries the shaky/missed split nothing else on the
page carries -- but it was fixed, so "probes graded" extracted from the 2-page PDF on pages
**[1,2]**; un-fixed, it flows once, and re-measured it is on page **[2]**.

`print-qa.js:109`'s home early-return is untouched, as ruled.

### T2 -- THE DOUBLE ANNOUNCEMENT

Cycle 4's item 5 closed a DIVERGENCE by composing both channels from `goalLine()`. That was right,
and making them identical is what made the real defect audible. Read off the CDP accessibility tree

BEFORE                                                       AFTER

tree as text, the two steppers keep their own names, `.ix-goal-t` keeps its `aria-live`.

## 5. CARRIED ITEMS

Five, all RECORDED with the measurements a successor needs, none of them open work.

1. **A cram sheet can be left OPEN on the home, and the home now prints itself underneath it.**
   New with T1. Both doors driven. Before T1 that state printed the sheet alone; after T1 it prints
   the home AND the sheet. What print should do when a dialog is open over a route that prints fine
   on its own is a product call, not a bug fix.
2. **The phone home paints ~57px scrolled for a beat on some loads.** The Resume CTA's
   `data-autofocus="1"` scrolls it into view before layout settles; it returns to 0 within a second.
   Measured at 2 of 12 loads. `home_fold` is immune (it pins the scroll and asserts it) and VR is
   not exposed (`stableShot` needs two consecutive byte-identical frames). The honest fix is a
   decision about whether autofocus should scroll at all on a route whose first screen IS the
   deliverable. It bit once more from the other side in cycle 4: a probe clicking by raw
   coordinates missed after `router.js`'s `pinTop` timers pulled the page back -- a PROBE defect,
   chased to the bottom, re-driven with `locator.click()`.
3. **The chip list is never the full-containment carrier in any shape this repo measures** -- 22
   cells across 390x844 and 360x844. The ruled contract holds in every one of them via the ACT.
   Closing the chip half would need the `.ix-cross` bars compacted, which R1 explicitly forbade.
4. **`q` and `n` are not separately driven in the boot window.** One gate closes every key, so one
   arm is aimed at the gate (`p`, plus `w` as the highest-signal navigator). If a future edit made
   the gate per-key again, this arm would still pass for `q`.
5. **The boot-window arm's shipping form HOLDS the window open.** The natural window is driven too,
   but as EVIDENCE (3 real boots, bounded at 4s, count printed) rather than as the assertion --
   it lands 4 times in 6, and a miss under the ruling's own predicate costs a 120s timeout. If a
   future change widens the natural window, the honest move is to promote the natural arm and
   retire the hold, not to add a second hold.

## 6. APPEARANCE REGISTER -- what the home LOOKS like now that it did not

Put the branch-point capture beside the freeze capture (`_audit/w15-receipts/`, light) and the
change is one idea applied five times: **the record says each thing once, in the place where it can
be acted on.**

The sidebar lost its "THIS WEEK" block -- a second goal bar and a second "0 of 5 topics", 261x51
px of duplicate under the Practice nav. The rail now ends where its navigation ends, and the
weekly goal appears once, in the work column, inside the panel whose head already names the period.
That panel used to be **engaged-only**, so a first-run user saw no goal at all; it is on every
record class now, which is what put the rest of this wave's goal work in front of every user. Its
kicker went with it (the head said "This week" and the line under it said "This week's goal" --
101x7 px of the same word twice), and the panel stopped sitting as a 248px island centred in a
624px row, because `margin:0 auto` on a grid item is not centring, it is shrinking.

The one thing that got BIGGER is the only thing a finger touches: the `- 5 +` stepper went from a
20px chip to a 44px target, and the group reads as one control rather than three floating glyphs.
On the desktop that cost the panel 18px; on the phone it SAVED 6, because the element floor already
made that row 44px tall. The focus ring followed the chip rather than staying on the reserved box,
where it had been drawing a 68px halo straight through the number it sets and the bar it fills.

On the phone -- the viewport three of the four cycles were spent on -- the two practice acts moved
from **2,136px down the page to 360px**, above the gauge rather than below the gauge, both panels
and all six room cards; the gauge itself lost 105px of its own padding and rails, and the
Still-shaky chips came back inside the first screen on the tight record shapes. The gauge's
four-state key stayed, after being measured at 21px of a 742px band and judged not worth the only
legend those marks have.

And the home prints as the home. Ctrl+P on the record used to emit a cram sheet for a topic the
user never chose; it emits two pages of the record now -- the hero, the gauge with all three rails
and its key, the week, all six rooms, the list and the census -- with the rail, the tab bar and the
preference checkbox left off the paper, because a fixed bar repeats on every sheet and a checkbox
means nothing there.
