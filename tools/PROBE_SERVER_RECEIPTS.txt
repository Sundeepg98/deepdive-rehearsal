4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE
---------------------------

  - It does not prove parity under LOAD. Both passes ran on a quiet box. The claim is
    value parity, plus a timing floor; a loaded box changes the timings, not the values.
  - It does not prove every possible measurement agrees -- 15 representative ones do.
    A measurement that depends on wall-clock time, on animation phase, or on anything
    the app persists across a session is exactly where warm and cold could still part
    company, which is why /reload builds a NEW CONTEXT rather than just navigating.
  - It does not make the server safe to use carelessly: the contamination discipline is
    what keeps a fast probe honest, and section 3 is the proof the guard fires, not a
    promise that nobody will pass mode:"explore" to something that deserved a reload.
